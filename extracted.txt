 SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT
 Agreement No. SSA/2027/0142
 
This Software Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 15 December 2026 and takes effect
on 1 January 2027 (the "Effective Date"), between:
(1) ACME RETAIL PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in India with its registered office
at 14 Park Street, Pune, Maharashtra 411001 (the "Client"); and
(2) BRIGHTSOFT TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in India with
its registered office at 22 Tech Park Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560001 (the "Vendor").
The Client and the Vendor are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".
1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Services" means the software development, hosting and maintenance services described in
Clause 2 and Schedule A.
1.2 "Term" means the Initial Term and every Renewal Term under Clause 3.
1.3 "Deliverables" means all software, code, documentation and other materials created by the
Vendor for the Client under this Agreement.
1.4 "Fees" means the amounts payable by the Client under Clause 4.
1.5 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Maharashtra.
2. SERVICES
2.1 The Vendor shall design, develop, host and maintain an inventory management system for the
Client's retail stores (the "System"), in accordance with the specifications and service levels set out
in Schedule A.
2.2 The Vendor shall deliver the first working version of the System within 90 days of the Effective
Date (the "Go-Live Date"). If the Vendor fails to meet the Go-Live Date, the Client's sole remedy is
a reduction of 2% of the first quarterly Fee for each full week of delay, up to a maximum of 10%.
2.3 The Vendor shall provide email and telephone support on Business Days between 9:00 a.m. and
6:00 p.m. India Standard Time. Critical faults shall be acknowledged within 4 hours and resolved
within 2 Business Days.
2.4 Any work outside the scope of Schedule A shall be treated as a change request and charged at
INR 4,500 per person-day, subject to the Client's prior written approval.
3. TERM AND RENEWAL
3.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of 12 months
(the "Initial Term").
3.2 At the end of the Initial Term, and of each Renewal Term, this Agreement renews automatically
for a further period of 12 months (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party gives the other
written notice of non-renewal at least 7 days before the end of the then-current term.
3.3 On each renewal the Vendor may increase the Fees by up to 10% by giving the Client written
notice at any time before the renewal date.
4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Client shall pay the Vendor a fee of INR 8,00,000 (Rupees eight lakh) per quarter, exclusive
of GST, payable quarterly in advance.
4.2 The Vendor shall invoice the Client at the start of each quarter. Each invoice is due for payment
within 15 days of the invoice date.
4.3 Late payments shall carry interest at the rate of 3% per month (or part of a month) from the due
date until the date of actual payment.
4.4 If any invoice remains unpaid for more than 7 days after its due date, the Vendor may suspend
the Services without notice until all overdue amounts, including interest, are paid in full.
4.5 All Fees are non-refundable, except as expressly stated in Clause 2.2.
4.6 The Client shall also reimburse the Vendor for reasonable travel and accommodation expenses
incurred in connection with on-site visits requested by the Client.
5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
5.1 All Deliverables, and all intellectual property rights in them, shall remain the sole and exclusive
property of the Vendor, whether or not the Client has paid for their development.
5.2 The Vendor grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable licence to use
the Deliverables for the Client's internal business purposes during the Term only.
5.3 On expiry or termination of this Agreement for any reason, the Client's licence ends
immediately and the Client shall stop using the Deliverables.
5.4 The Client retains ownership of the data it enters into the System ("Client Data"). The Vendor
may use anonymised Client Data to improve its products and services.
6. WARRANTIES
6.1 The Vendor warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care.
6.2 The Vendor does not warrant that the System will be error-free or uninterrupted. Except as
stated in Clause 6.1, all warranties and conditions, whether express or implied by law, are excluded
to the fullest extent permitted.
6.3 The Client warrants that it has the right to provide the Client Data and that its use of the System
will comply with applicable law.
7. TERMINATION
7.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party 30 days'
written notice.
7.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party
commits a material breach that is not remedied within 15 days of written notice requiring it to be
remedied.
7.3 The Vendor may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if any invoice remains
unpaid for more than 14 days after its due date. The Client has no equivalent right.
7.4 On termination, the Client shall pay all Fees due up to the date of termination. No Fees paid in
advance shall be refunded.
7.5 On termination, the Vendor shall make the Client Data available for download for 14 days, after
which the Vendor may delete it.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
8.1 The Vendor's total liability to the Client arising out of or in connection with this Agreement,
whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the Fees paid by the
Client in the 3 months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
8.2 The Vendor shall not be liable for any indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, loss of
revenue, loss of data or loss of goodwill, even if advised of the possibility of such loss.
8.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by
negligence.
9. INDEMNITY
9.1 The Client shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Vendor and its officers and employees
from and against all claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses (including legal fees) arising out of
or in connection with the Client's use of the System or the Client Data. The Client's liability under
this Clause is not subject to any limit.
9.2 The Vendor shall have no obligation to indemnify the Client for any claim that the Deliverables
infringe the intellectual property rights of a third party.
10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
10.2 The courts of Singapore shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter arising out of or in
connection with this Agreement.
11. NOTICES
11.1 A notice under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by email to the address of the
receiving Party stated on the signature page.
11.2 A notice sent by email is treated as received at the time of sending, unless the sender receives
an automated message that the email has not been delivered.
12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBCONTRACTING
12.1 The Client may not assign, transfer or subcontract any of its rights or obligations under this
Agreement without the Vendor's prior written consent.
12.2 The Vendor may assign this Agreement, or subcontract any part of the Services, to any third
party at any time without the Client's consent.
13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
13.1 This Agreement, including Schedule A, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its
subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions, proposals and understandings.
13.2 No amendment to this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both Parties.
